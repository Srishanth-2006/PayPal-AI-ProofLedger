## 💡 Practical Voiceover Recording Tips

1. **Watch and Speak:** Open the video in your media player (VLC, Windows Media Player) and read the script along with the video.
2. **Natural Pauses:** The video has natural 2 to 3-second breathing pauses between scenes. You don't need to rush!
3. **If You Stumble:** Just pause, re-read the sentence, and trim the mistake in your video editor (CapCut, Clipchamp, Premiere, or DaVinci).
4. **Tone:** Keep your tone confident, enthusiastic, and clear. Judges love hearing a creator who is proud of their engineering!
